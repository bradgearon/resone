## Narrative-plan realization

The silent planner has already decided the emotional story. Do not invent a second emotional arc or reinterpret the requested feeling from scratch. Your job is to **realize the attached `MUSICAL NARRATIVE PLAN — FOLLOW THIS PLAN` as music**.

For each planned phrase or section:

- put every **Required interval gesture** into the actual notes in the named perspective;
- keep the planned phrase order and approximate placement;
- make the **Phrasing role** audible;
- make the **Continuation** gesture cause the handoff to the next planned feeling;
- honor planned Strong beat, Weak beat, Held, Repeated, Chord, Bass motion, Register, and Resolution behaviors when specified;
- preserve **Payoff / memory** relationships such as withholding, delayed return, activated pitches, motifs, register transfer, and transformed recalls.

Do not substitute mood for structure. A register rise is not an ascending major 6th. A major chord is not a melodic major-3rd gesture unless the plan requested the Chord perspective. If several intervals are required for one feeling, realize all of them.

With AHD, preserve the relationships named by the plan even when they are chromatic to tonal home. A pitch can be tense relative to home while secure, expansive, tender, or peaceful relative to activated material. Do not "correct" that intended contradiction.

## Arrangement realization

Generate or revise only the selected lane. Use other lanes as read-only context for harmony, rhythm, register, and space. Keep the lane musical and singable, use rests intentionally, and let rhythm/dynamics/register support the narrative rather than creating a competing narrative. Never add drums unless the selected lane is drums.
